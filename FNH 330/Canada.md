# Canada
- mostly [[British Columbia]], [[Ontario]], [[Nova Scotia]]
	- [[Quebec]] produces wine but the conditions are very difficult
## [[History of Wine]] in Canada
- "jug wine" era (pre-1988 [[North America Free Trade Agreement]])
	- *V. labrusca*, American hybrids, French hybrids, few *V. vinifera* plantings
- post [[North America Free Trade Agreement|NAFTA]] (1988)
	- Canadian market opened to US wines
	- [[Ontario]] and [[British Columbia|BC]] began to replant with *V. vinifera* varieties to improve quality
	- currently over 28 000 acres of vineyard
- improving Canadian wine quality
	- in [[Ontario]], [[Wine Content Act]] imposed minimum quality standards
	- estate wineries formed [[Vintners Quality Alliance]] in 1989
	- 1990, under the [[BC Wine Act]], BC government created [[BC-VQA|BC-Vintners Quality Alliance]]
		- minimum standards for BC wines (100% BC grapes)
## Canadian Wine Classifications
 - [[International blend of imported and domestic wines]]
	 - imported wine, blended with domestic
	 - no origin labels required
- [[100% Canadian wine]]
	- made in regions without [[Vintners Quality Alliance|VQA]] system
	- wines made outside [[Vintners Quality Alliance|VQA]]
- [[Vintners Quality Alliance]] wines
	- quality regulated wines with a general geographic indication
		- e.g. [[BC-VQA]] or [[Vintners Quality Alliance|VQA]] (Ontario)
	- can have **specific geographic indications**
		- e.g. [[Niagara Peninsula]]
		- [[Okanagan Valley]]
		- [[Shuswap]]
		- [[Lillooet]]
	- equivalent to [[Protected Designation of Origin|PDO]] wines in EU
