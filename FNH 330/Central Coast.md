# Central Coast
- [[Mediterranean Climate|Mediterranean]]-[[Maritime Climate]]
- regional [[Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau|AVA]] in [[California]] containing many smaller AVAs
- [[Winkler Index]] region 1-3
	- cooler closer to ocean
- from a bit south of SF to LA
- northern part of the region
	- some valleys run parallel to the coast
	- coastal climate
		- low rainfall but foggy, windy, cool conditions
			- may need irrigation
		- long growing season = maintain acidity/fruit
	- [[Syrah]] and [[Zinfandel]]
	- [[San Luis Obispo County]]
		- [[Paso Robles]] [[Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau|AVA]]
			- hot and dry
		- [[Edna Valley]] [[Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau|AVA]]
			- marine cooling is strong
- south part of the region
	- valleys run more east to west
	- [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]
