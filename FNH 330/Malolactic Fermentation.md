# Malolactic Fermentation
- second fermentation performed by bacteria *Oenococcus oeni*
- can be inoculated or spontaneous
	- spontaneous MLF by other lactic acid bacteria (LAB) may make sauerkraut aromas
- MLF converts malic acid to lactic acid + CO$_2$
	- reduces harsh acidity; increases pH
	- helps stabilize wine (nutrients consumed)
	- avoids off-aromas
