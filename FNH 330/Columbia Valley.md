# Columbia Valley
- main winemaking regional AVA of [[Washington]]
- hot, sunny summers
- cool in winter
- [[Continental Climate|Continental]]-semi-desert
	- relies on irrigation from Columbia river basin
- big shift in summer/winter temps
- contains many AVAs
	- [[Red Mountain]]
	- [[Horse Heaven Hills]]
	- [[Yakima Valley]]
