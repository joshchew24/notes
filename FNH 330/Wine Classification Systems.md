# Wine Classification Systems
## Early Attempts
- as early as 1300s in France
- most influential was [[Grands crus classés|1855 Bordeaux classification]]
	- big marketing success
	- regulation of these wines is very tightly maintained
	- nowadays, these wines are very expensive
## Champagne (early 1900s)
- several poor vintages, phylloxera, conflict between growers and Champagne houses
- with limited grape supply, Champagne houses began to purchase cheap wine from southern regions
- **1911**: Champagne growers were upset and rioted
	- military came to restore order
- **1927**
	- after years of negotiation, French government established that "Champagne" sparkling wine must be made from grapes grown in Champagne
	- established new Champagne appellation
	- Champagne began to apply for legal restrictions on use of "Champagne" term
