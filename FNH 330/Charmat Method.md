# Charmat Method
- alternative [[Sparkling Wine]] making method to [[Champagne Winemaking Steps|Champagne/Traditional Method]]
## Compared to [[Champagne Winemaking Steps|Champagne Method]]
- [[Secondary Fermentation]] and [[Optional Steps - Post-fermentation#Sur Lie|sur-lie]] aging in tank instead of bottles
	- much easier, but quality considered to be lower
		- mixers mounted on tanks stir [[Lees]] to facilitate contact
		- wines are filtered to clarify
	- weeks to 9 months instead of years
	- smaller ratio surface area of [[Lees]] in contact with volume of liquid
	- e.g. [[Prosecco]]
