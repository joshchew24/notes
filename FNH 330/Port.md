# Port
- typical [[Aromas]]: cooked fruit, cherries, licorice
- **red, dessert** wine
	- [[Red Winemaking Steps]]
- type of [[Fortified wine]] from Portugal
- common varieties: [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Francesca]], [[Tempranillo]]
	- sometimes varieties are mixed randomly in vineyards, harvested and fermented together
		- in old vineyards, varieties may be unknown
	- modern trend: vineyards grow one variety
		- fermented separately and blended
- must be [[Grape Harvesting#Hand Harvesting|hand-picked]]
	- vineyards in Portugal have steep slopes
- traditionally [[Crushing|crushed]] by foot
	- common to use special mechanical crushers
- [[Fermentation]]
	- closed or open tanks at 26-32ºC
	- **fortified** with **brandy spirits** (70% ABV) when still sweet (~6% ABV)
		- brings wine's ABV to ~20%
		- stops the fermentation
	- [[Pressing#Red Wine]] and [[Blending]]
## Port Styles
- all are port but have processing/quality differences
	- aging differences
### Ruby
- aged for 2 years
- considered economy
### Vintage
- premium
- all grapes from same year
	- made in years where growing season was optimal
- aged 2-3 years in barrels/pipes before [[Bottling]]
	- pipes: large oak vessels
- aged in bottle before release
	- long period of time
### Late Bottled Vintage Port
- instead of aging in bottle, aged in barrels/pipes for 4-6 years
	- oxidizes it, ages more quickly
- near premium
- ready to drink on release
### Tawny Port
- aged in barrels/pipes 10-40 years
- premium
- not necessarily one vintage
- amber/red-brown (oxidized) on release
	- nutty/maple flavours
	- dried fruit aromas
