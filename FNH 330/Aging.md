# Aging
## Red Wine
- 9 to 24+ months
	- longer than whites
- done in both barrels & tanks
	- then blended
### Nouveau wines
- tanks/vats only
- sold 9 weeks post-harvest
- e.g. Beaujolais Nouevau
	- bottled and released weeks after fermentation
### Bulk reds
- aged with oak chips in tanks
	- cheaper than oak barrel
- relatively cheap
- aged ~9 months before release
- marketed 9 months post-harvest
### Quality Reds
- AOC Bordeaux/Burgundy
	- Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée 
- aged minimum 6 to 24+ months in **barrels**
	- often an additional year in bottle
	- some lower AOC Bordeaux aged in tanks/old barrels
## Barrel Aging
- oxidative aging
	- soften wine texture
		- lowers astringency of tannins
	- stabilize colour
	- oak wood is **porous**
		- allows oxygen transfer
		- influences wine sensory profile
