# Bordeaux
- ![[Pasted image 20250413204810.png]]
- main allowed grape varieties:
	- [[Merlot]]
	- [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]
	- [[Cabernet Franc]]
	- [[Malbec]]
	- [[Petit Verdot]]
	- [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
	- [[Semillon]]
- highest producing wine region in [[France]]
- Climate:
	- Moderate [[Maritime Climate|Maritime]] 
	- EU Zone CI - warm
	- no frost, mild winters
	- excess rain is main risk factor, especially during harvest
		- bloated grapes (lower concentration)
		- disease
	- [[Saignée]] may be used in some vintages to increase concentration
- [[Soil]]s
	- left bank: **Haut-medoc**
		- [[Grands crus classés]]
		- coarse and gravelly - good drainage
			- key to early spring warming and longer growing season
				- good for [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]
	- **Bas-Medoc**
		- silty soil, not known for quality wine
	- right bank
		- clay + silt soil over limestone
		- slower drainage, cooler soil
			- good for [[Merlot]]
## Classifications
### Chateau Winery System
- chateaus **own** vineyards
	- grow own grapes on estate, vinify and bottle wine
	- i.e. control everything but retail sales
- > 100,000 ha of vineyards and > 3000 Chateaus
- left bank: [[Grands crus classés|1855 Bordeaux classification]]
	- best of the best
### St-Émilion 1955 Classification
- for right bank chateaus
- 1er grand cru classe a (highest) & b, plus many grands crus classés
- Note: Petrus (2005: $4975) was never classified
- non-cru-classified areas planned out their own cru classé systems within AOC
- **Graves** followed with a new grand cru classification in **1959**
### Cru bourgeois
- used **just beneath** [[Grands crus classés]] system
- established in 1932, revised in 2010, 2020
- meant to **distinguish** the chateaus making good quality wines above the above-average, but not as good as [[Grands crus classés|Grand cru classé]]
