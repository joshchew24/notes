# Disgorgement
- method to remove **plug** created by [[Riddling]]
- can be done mechanically
1. chill wine to 0ºC to keep CO$_2$ dissvoled in wine during disgorgement
	- if not, gas will escape
2. tip bottle upside down
3. dip cap/neck/opening in freezing brine (-15ºC) (around 1 inch)
4. solidifies/freezes plug and wine at opening
5. tilt bottle at 45º angle and remove cap
6. CO$_2$ ejects frozen plug
