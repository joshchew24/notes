# 100% Canadian wine
- *V. vinifera*, but French/American hybrids allowed
	- Vidal, Marechal FOch
- made in regions where no formal [[Vintners Quality Alliance|VQA]] system is implemented, or wines made outside of VQA system
	- Nova Scotia, Quebec
- may have additional terms
	- e.g. "Wine of Nova Scotia"
