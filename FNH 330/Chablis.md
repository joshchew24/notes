# Chablis
- northern tip of [[Burgundy]]
- EU Wine Growing Zone B
- [[Cool Climate|Cool]] [[Continental Climate]]
- chalky soils
- best known for [[Chardonnay]]
- best makers use little or no **oak** that might mask **variety** and [[Terroir]]
- wines from the Grand cru are produced from **sloped, south-facing** vineyards
	- intercept the most light and heat
