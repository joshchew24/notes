# Red Winemaking Steps
1. [[Sorting]] and [[Destemming]]
	- destemmed -> sorted (triage) on conveyor to remove debris & unsuitable grapes
2. [[Crushing]]
	- skins come in contact with juice
3. [[Pectinase Enzyme Addiction after Crushing]]
4. Must pumpted into fermenter and [[Cold Soak]]
	- skins and seeds not racked off like for white wines (see [[Preparing the Must - Juice Clarification]])
	- [[Pre-fermentation Adjustments]]
	- add dry ice to tanks to create anaerobic condition
		- CO$_2$
		- when the dry ice sublimates, it displaces the oxygen in the tank to prevent spoilage
5. [[Fermentation]]
6. [[Racking]]
	- once grape must fermented to ~40 g/L sugar (4˚ [[Brix]])
		- some wineries may ferment to dryness
		- some wineries may leave wine on the skins for extended period of time to macerate
			- extracts more tannins
7. [[Pressing]]
	- cap from racking saved for pressing
		- treated gently to avoid extracting vegetal flavours
8. [[Malolactic Fermentation]]
9. [[Aging]]
10. [[Barrel Top-up]] and [[Racking]]
	- important to prevent [[Spoilage]]
	- also monitoring [[Sulfur Dioxide and Sorbic Acid]] levels
11. [[Fining]] and Clarification
	- once aging is complete
	- less fining than whites
12. [[Bottling]] and Storage, bottle aging
