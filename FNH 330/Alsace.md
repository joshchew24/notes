# Alsace
- is an [[Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée|AOC]]
- Climate
	- EU Zone B
	- [[Cool Climate|Cool]] ([[Continental Climate|Continental]]) with a [[Unique Sub-Climates#Cool Climate Rain shadow|Rain Shadow]] influence
		- Vosges Mountains protect the region from inclement weather systems coming in from Atlantic Ocean
		- slightly warmer/drier climate
- main ("**noble**") grape varieties
	- [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewurztraminer]], [[Muscat]]
	- some [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]
- other wines
	- Vendanges Tardives ([[Late Harvest Wines]])
	- Selections de Grains Nobles ([[Late Harvest Wines|Late Harvest]]  [[Botrytis-affected Wines]])
## Grand Cru System
- "Alsace Grand Cru AOC"
- different from [[Grands crus classés]]
- awarded to **vineyards**
- established in 1983
- **restrictions**
	- yield amount
	- wines must be made from one of the **noble grape** varieties
## Alsace AOC
- less restrictive
- [[Pre-fermentation Adjustments|Chaptalization]] allowed with lower quality AOC wines
- higher yields (75-100 hL/ha)
