# Pfalz
- 23% of Germany's wine
	- 2nd largest producer (to [[Rheinhessen]])
- climate:
	- has a [[Unique Sub-Climates#Cool Climate Rain shadow Rain Shadow|Rain Shadow]] from [[Haardt Mountains]]
		- extension of [[Vosges Mountains]]
	- more sunshine, less rain than would be expected in zone A
- **grape varieties**:
	- [[Riesling]] 25%
	- [[Dornfelder]] (red) 12%
	- [[Pinot Gris]], [[Muller Thurgau]], [[Pinot Noir|Spätburgunder]]
