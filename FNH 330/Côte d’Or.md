# Côte d’Or
- AKA Golden Slope, East SLope
- Zone CI
- 2 major sub regions
### Côte de Nuits
- north part of Côte d’Or
- best wines (mainly [[Pinot Noir]]) are from the Grand cru vineyards
	- southeast facint, variably sloped in that direction
	- e.g. Romanée-Conti, Chambertin
		- Romanée-Conti: only a few thousand bottles of wine a year ($5-10 thousand a bottle)
- sometimes [[Chardonnay]]
### Côte de Beaune
- south of Côte de Nuits
- [[Pinot Noir]], but famous for [[Chardonnay]]
	- dry, flinty, toasty
- pre-eminent [[Chardonnay]] vineyards
	- [[Corton-Charlemagne]], [[Montrachet]]
- most successful vineyards slope to south-southeast
	- sunshine hours and heat units are greatest
	- at mid-slope, drainage is best with a thin soil layer so vegetative growth is not excessive
