# Specialty Wines
- **still**, **sweet** white/red, med-high ABV (aperitif or dessert)
	- 8-21%
- style is determined by **harvest criteria** and **processing method**
	- late harvest
	- dried grapes
	- *Botrytis*-affected
		- [[Aromas]]: apricot, lychee, honey
	- frozen grapes
	- variable fermentation methods
		- e.g. fortified
