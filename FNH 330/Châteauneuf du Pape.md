# Châteauneuf du Pape
- in the driest area of the Rhône valley
- vineyards facing [[Mistral]] winds
- 13 red and white grape varieties permitted
	- mainly [[Grenache]] and [[Mourvèdre]], [[Syrah]], small amounts of [[Cinsault]], [[Muscardin]], [[Counoise]], [[Clariette]], [[Bourboulenc]]
- regulations **stricter** than most AOCs in [[France]]
	- low yields, mandatory hand harvesting, [[Sorting|Triage]], minimum ABV, vine density
- [[Rosé]] is banned
