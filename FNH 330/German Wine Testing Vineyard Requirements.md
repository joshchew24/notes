# German Wine Testing Vineyard Requirements
- juice sugar concentration, mold (minimum of botrytis for BA (50%) & TBA (100% BA) wines), yield level estimated
## Beerenauslese Example
### Component 1
the sugar level of the grape juice for the proposed Beerenauslese wine and, as this is a high quality late-harvest botrytis-affected wine, the grape juice must reach the minimum high specific gravity/Oechsle level (about 110-128ºOechsle) required for this style of wine (compare with Kabinett which would be the lowest level (minimum 67 Oechsle for a Pradikatswein).
### Component 2
as Beerenauslese is a late-harvest botrytis-affected wine – grapes must all be botrytis-affected and have a minimum amount of drying (average about 50%). 
### Component 3
Beerenauslese - a late-harvest botrytis-affected wine – is expensive as it is difficult to produce and of high quality. Estimation of the amount of botrytis-affected grapes allows an estimation of bottle production - this can be checked against actual bottles sold. Hence the wine authority can ensure that a winery is not mixing in lower quality wine/sugar in order to increase its overall bottles and hence its sales income.

## More Examples
FT-i: concentration of sugar in the grape juice

FT-ii: minimum level of botrytis affectation for Beerenauslese (50%) and Trockenbeerenauslese (100%) wines

FT-iii: yield level estimated using amount of grapes to actual bottles sold. Helps ensure that higher quality wines have not been mixed with lower quality wine to increase bottles sold. 

#### Wine 1: Riesling Kabinett

FT-i: the sugar level of the grape juice that was used to make this wine must reach a minimum Oechsle level of 75º, since it is a Kabinett wine. 

FT-ii: since this wine is not botrytis affected, it has no requirement for mold. It probably should not exceed 50%, which is the minimum amount required for a Beerenauslese wine.

FT-iii: since the wine is considered lower quality, it probably has a higher allowed yield amount. The grapes are processed while more fresh and lower concentration, yielding more juice to make into wine.

#### Wine 2: Riesling Trockenbeerenauselese

FT-i: the sugar level of the grape juice used to make a Trockenbeerenauselese wine is the highest of all Pradikatswein tiers. The concentration must exceed 150º Oechsle, which is the amount required for Eiswein. 

FT-ii: the grapes used to make this wine must be 100% botrytis-affected

FT-iii: the allowable yield of this wine is the lowest of all Pradikatswein tiers, because the grapes are the most botrytis-affected, and have a much higher sugar concentration, yielding less juice
