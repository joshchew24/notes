# German Wine Testing Vineyard Requirements
- juice sugar concentration, mold (minimum of botrytis for BA (50%) & TBA (100% BA) wines), yield level estimated
## Beerenauslese Example
### Component 1
the sugar level of the grape juice for the proposed Beerenauslese wine and, as this is a high quality late-harvest botrytis-affected wine, the grape juice must reach the minimum high specific gravity/Oechsle level (about 110-128ºOechsle) required for this style of wine (compare with Kabinett which would be the lowest level (minimum 67 Oechsle for a Pradikatswein).
### Component 2
as Beerenauslese is a late-harvest botrytis-affected wine – grapes must all be botrytis-affected and have a minimum amount of drying (average about 50%). 
### Component 3
Beerenauslese - a late-harvest botrytis-affected wine – is expensive as it is difficult to produce and of high quality. Estimation of the amount of botrytis-affected grapes allows an estimation of bottle production - this can be checked against actual bottles sold. Hence the wine authority can ensure that a winery is not mixing in lower quality wine/sugar in order to increase its overall bottles and hence its sales income.
