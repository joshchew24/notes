# Meritage
- wine style term from [[California]]
- used in North America
- premium wine using varietel blends (2 or more)
	- style similar to [[Bordeaux]]
	- no one variety can make up more than 90% of the blend
- i.e. [[Bordeaux]] style blends
## Red Meritage
- mainly [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
	- [[Bordeaux]] grapes
- also [[Petit Verdot]], [[Malbec]], [[Carmenere]]
## White Meritage
- [[Semillon]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], small amount of [[Muscadelle]]
