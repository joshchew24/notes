# Prädikatswein
- [[Protected Designation of Origin|Special PDO]]
- old term: Qualitätswein mit Pradikat (QmP)
- 6 tiers based on grape sugars
- Kabinett (least sweet) -> Spatlese -> Auslese -> [[Botrytis-affected Wines|Beerenauslese]] -> [[Icewine|Eiswein]] -> Trockenbeerenauslese (most sweet)
## Restrictions
- only certain varieties permitted
- each wine "grade" is made from grapes that meet that grade's minimum sugar level
- required grape sugar level/ripeness increases with grade
## Tiers
### Prädikatswein Kabinett
- normal harvest (e.g. early October), after [[Qualitätswein]]
- usually off-dry
	- if high ABV% and labelled [[Trocken]] = dry
### Prädikatswein Spätlese
- harvested later, with full varietal character
- usually off-dry
	- if high ABV% and labelled [[Trocken]] = dry
### Prädikatswein Auslese
- harvested even later, very ripe bunches selected
	- bit of [[Botrytis-affected Wines|BA]] allowed
- Auslese starts the "[[Late Harvest Wines|Late Harvest]]" dessert-style wine series
### Prädikatswein [[Botrytis-affected Wines|Beerenauslese]] (BA)
- similar to Auslese but [[Botrytis-affected Wines]]
	- all grapes shrivelled about 50% by [[Botrytis cinerea|Edelfaule]]
### Prädikatswein [[Icewine|Eiswein]]
- harvested after frozen at maximum high of -7ºC
- extract sweet concentrated juice from ripe, relatively healthy grapes
### Prädikatswein [[Trockenbeerenauslese]] (TBA)
- sweetest [[Must]], highest quality
- "dry, sweet berries" almost raisined
	- similary to [[Botrytis-affected Wines|Beerenauslese]], but syrupy
