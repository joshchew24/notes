# Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée
- AKA Appelation d’origine protégée
- on [[Bottle Labels]]
	- Appellation \<region\> contrôlée/protégée
- usually doesn't have varietal labelling
	- [[Institut national des appellations d'origine|INAO]] restricts what varieties are grown in each region, so it is implied
	- only exception is [[Alsace]]
