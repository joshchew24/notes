# Assemblage
- blending of the component wines for [[Champagne]]
## Non-vintage
- compiling many diverse wines for blending of the “cuvée”
	- reserve wines (i.e. other vintages)
		- can be >30% of total blend
		- more complex flavours due to aging
		- allows for consistency in flavours
			- many diverse options to choose from to achieve target "style"
			- same vintages across many years of champagne production provides consistency
	- estate wines
		- wines from own grapes
	- contracted wines
		- wine from purchased grapes
## Vintage
- if current year's grapes have **exceptional quality**
	- processing and ageing is extended
- compiling of vintage wines into **cuvée**
	- estate and contracted wines
- cuvée undergoes [[Cold stabilization]] prior to next steps
