# Wine and Health
- historically believed to be good for your health
## Possible Beneficial Compounds
- [[Phenolic Compounds]] may be beneficial
	- tannins, flavonoids, pigments
	- Quercetin
		- antioxidant
		- less low density lipoprotein (LDL) oxidation/plaque formation
			- may help prevent blood clots
		- reduces inflammation
		- anti-cancer agent
		- may contribute to wine headaches?
	- Resveratrol
		- reduces inflammation
		- lowers LDL
		- may also help prevent blood clots
		- promotes increase in good cholesterol (high density lipoprotein - HDL)
	- Epicatechins
		- anti-cancer agents
		- may also help prevent blood clots
## California "J" Curve
- study published in 1981 by A. L. Klatsky
- suggests that a moderate intake of wine **every day** reduces risk of premature death
- zero and excessive intakes were associated with a higher risk
