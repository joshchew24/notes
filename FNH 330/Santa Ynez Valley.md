# Santa Ynez Valley
- hotter than [[Santa Maria Valley]]
- [[AVA]] in [[Santa Barbara County]], [[Central Coast|Central Coast AVA]]
- vineyards planted on north-facing slopes
- foggy, cool westerlies offset hot sun
- [[Santa Rita Hills]] block coldest winds
- mainly [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Zinfandel]]
- irrigation is essential since rainfall is low
