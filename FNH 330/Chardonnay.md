# Chardonnay
- can be oaked
- slightly fruity (apple, melon, citrus, pineapple, but often muted with processing)
- occasionally a hint spicy or nutty (nutmeg or hazelnut) with oak; also vanilla, butter, cream, rarely cream corn. Chardonnay requires a relatively short-medium growing season
	- but it can be left on the vines in some terroir to good effect (more pineapple)
- Somewhat neutral, it lends itself well to the Champagne process, or malolactic fermentation, lees stirring and barrel maturation (big oaky California style), or even to a late harvest, dessert wine style. Chablis (coolfermentation and fruit preservation i.e. little oak).
