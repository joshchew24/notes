# Bottling
## Bottling
- wine bottled in glass
	- rinsed and filled with nitrogen to protect against oxidation
	- or bags
- bottling closure:
	- optional cork or screw cap
	- depends on region, winemaker's choice
	- inert N$_2$ enclosure
		- no air for oxidation risk
- wine is bottled & labelled
	- may be held for labeling and again before being shipped to overcome [[Bottle Shock]]
## Red Wine
- wine may be held before sale
	- for bottle shock or aging
- **bottle bouquet**
	- aromas due to bottle aging
	- in addition to varietal aromas, oak bouquet, etc.
	- acids & alcohols -> esters
