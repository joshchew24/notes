# Ontario
- about 17000 acres
- Ontario Viticultural Areas have hot, humid summers and cold winters
- considered [[Continental Climate]], but Great Lakes moderate temperature
- varied [[Soil]]
	- clay and loam in [[Niagara Peninsula]]
	- clay-loam on limestone in [[Prince Edward County]]
	- sandy loam in [[Lake Erie North Shore]]
