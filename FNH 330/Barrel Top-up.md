# Barrel Top-up
- some alcohol and water evaporates from barrels
	- because wood is porous
	- air accumulates in head space
		- facilitates oxidation and microbial [[Spoilage]]
- at least once a month, top-up the barrel with more wine
	- lose 1-2L of wine per month
