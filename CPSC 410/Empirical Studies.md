# Empirical Studies
## Threats to Validity
### Construct Validity
- are we testing **what we mean to test**?
- are we **measuring the right thing**?
- is there a **clear connection** back to the **Research Questions?**
- did we misunderstand the theory/concepts we are working with?
- concepts being studied are being **measured correctly**
	- no fundamental misdesign
### Internal Validity
- 
- are the results **solely** due to our manipulations?
- are we **confident** that our **inferred causes** are **accurate?**
- are there **alternative explanations** to our results?
	- e.g. bias, uncontrolled factors
### External Validity
- are the conclusions justified **in general**?
- are our findings **generalizable**?
- to what extent are our results and causes likely to **generalise** to conclusions about the **original research questions**?
- how **representative** are the tasks and users of the research question in general?
- what are the results applicable to?
### Empirical Reliability/Reproducibility
- could this be **shown again**, and **how?**
- can the study be **duplicated**?
	- to check for similar results
	- reproduction studies can be used to reduce threats to internal validity in original study
## Risks
- could paritcipants be harmed
	- physically, emotionally, mentally, etc.
