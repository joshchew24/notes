# North Rhône Valley
- hot [[Continental Climate]] 
	- zone CI
	- strong drying mistrals (wind) that blow through the valley
		- often use [[Bilateral Cordon System]], [[Double Guyot System]], or [[Vine Management#Bush/Umbrella System|Bush System]] to protect vine against wind
- known for [[Syrah]] wines
## Main AOCs
- [[Hermitage]]
	- reds and whites
- [[Condrieu]]
	- [[Viognier]]
- [[Crozes-Hermitage]]
- [[Cornas]]
- [[Côte-Rôtie]]
- [[Saint-Joseph]]
