# Anbaugebiete
- anbau = agricultural, gebiet = district
- 13 districts for quality wine designations (wine production)
	- divided by climate and geology
	- [[Rheinhessen]], [[Pfalz]], [[Baden]], [[Mosel]], [[Rheingau]]
	- also [[Wurtemberg]], [[Franken]], [[Nahe]], [[Saale-Unstrut]], [[Ahr]], [[Mittelrhein]], [[Hessiche Bergstrasse]], and [[Saachsen]]
- [[Bereich]]s are districts within an Anbaugebiete
	- e.g. [[Johannisberg]] in [[Rheingau]]
- [[Grosslage]]: collective site of vineyards
- [[Einzellage]]: single vineyard
