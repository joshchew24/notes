# Having Evaluation
- see also [[Having]]
- is like a [[Selection Evaluation|Select]] that happens after an [[Aggregation Evaluation|Aggregation]] with [[Group By Evaluation|Group By]]
	- i.e. **filter the results** of a grouped aggregation by the **Having** condition
- e.g. $\eta_{count(distinct\text{ sname}) > 2}$
